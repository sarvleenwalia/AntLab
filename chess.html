How this works

Ants explore a small part of the game, with stronger trails on promising moves. Both players make replies during the search. A separate check catches immediate checkmates. This is a small search experiment, not a complete solution to chess.

Chess rules are handled by the open-source chess.js library. The move-search algorithm is in this project’s source.

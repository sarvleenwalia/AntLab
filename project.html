ABOUT THE PROJECT

Small experiments. Results you can check.

AntLab explores how groups of simple virtual agents search for answers.

The maze experiment

Change a maze, let the ants try it, and keep changes that make it harder. Compare that with random changes using the same number of tries. Download both mazes, ant runs and settings. Every cell stays reachable; the ants’ rules stay the same.

The chess experiment

Ants try short lines of legal chess moves. Promising attempts leave stronger trails for later ants. A separate check recognises immediate checkmates. This player can make poor choices and lose games; it does not solve chess.

What to record

Keep starting mazes, settings, raw results, successes, failures and dated build notes. Test several starting layouts before claiming one method is better.

Credits and limits

Chess rules use chess.js, included with its licence. Implementation is AI-assisted; document your own design decisions and tests. Ants are simulated. Physical-ant positions are labeled manually. Five ant runs on one maze are a small pilot, not proof of broader performance.
